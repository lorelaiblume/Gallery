A victory for mankind, with America at the center

The U.S., motivated by the concept of American exceptionalism and the humiliation of early losses to the Soviet Union, used the Apollo program to reassert its status on the global stage and fulfill its self-determined obligation to serve as a leader for the world. Through intentional motivation and widespread media coverage, the nation used space exploration and the moon landing as a demonstration of national capability and influence — portraying Apollo 11 as a triumph for all mankind, while reinforcing its own image as the driving force behind this milestone of human progress.
